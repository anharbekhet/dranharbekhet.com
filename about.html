Meet the author

Dr. Anhar Bekhet

Author of the Sakinah Stories™ series.

Photo coming soon

Assalamu alaikum! I'm Dr. Anhar Bekhet. I started Sakinah Stories at story nights for kids and their parents, because I wanted stories that teach Islamic values and real emotional skills while making everyone laugh.

Each book takes a real challenge kids face, like anger, growing up, or bedtime, and pairs it with what the Quran and Sunnah teach and a simple skill kids can practice. Sakinah means tranquility, the calm Allah sends down into the hearts of believers. That's what I hope every story brings to your home.

Story nights and workshops

I visit schools, masjids and homeschool groups for story nights and parent workshops, in person and online.
