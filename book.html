Story nights & workshops

Bring Sakinah Stories to your community

Interactive story nights for kids and practical, faith-rooted workshops for parents, led by Dr. Anhar Bekhet, doctor and author of the Sakinah Stories™ series.

What I offer

Ways to bring Sakinah Stories to your community

Every visit can be in person or online. Sessions can be adapted for any age group or topic.

Story Night

For
Kids ages 4 to 10, with their parents
Length
45 to 60 minutes

An interactive read-aloud of a Sakinah Stories book, with hand motions, ask-the-kids moments, discussion questions and a take-home printable for every family.

Great for: Masjid family nights, Islamic school assemblies, homeschool co-ops, Ramadan and Eid events

Parent Workshop

For
Parents and teachers
Length
60 to 90 minutes with Q&A

Practical, faith-rooted guidance from a doctor and author. Topics include big feelings and anger, talking to kids about puberty, and bedtime routines without battles.

Great for: Masjid halaqas, parent nights, Islamic school staff training, conferences

Girls' Growing-Up Session

For
Girls ages 9 to 13 and their mothers
Length
60 to 90 minutes, women only

A warm, judgment-free session built on the Sarah books. Body changes, first periods, worship and haya, with plenty of time for anonymous questions.

Great for: Islamic schools, masjid sisters' programs, homeschool groups

Classroom Visit

For
Pre-K to grade 5
Length
30 to 45 minutes per class

A story and short activity in your classroom, matched to the topic you're teaching, from emotions to manners to growing up.

Great for: Islamic schools and weekend schools
