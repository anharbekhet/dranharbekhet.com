For parents of children ages 0 to 12

Caring Discipline, the Sunnah Way

Who it's for
Parents, grandparents and caregivers
Length
60 to 90 minutes with Q&A
Format
In person or online

Discipline means teaching, not punishing. Drawing on how the Prophet ﷺ guided children with mercy, patience and clear limits, this workshop looks at what's happening in a child's brain during a meltdown and what parents can do in the moment and afterward.

What we'll explore

  • How the brain's alarm works, with Amy the Amygdala and Professor Cortex
  • Staying calm yourself first, so children can borrow your calm
  • The Prophet's ﷺ calm-down plan: sit, seek refuge, stay quiet, make wudu
  • Connecting before correcting, and setting limits with warmth
  • Practical routines for bedtime and other everyday flashpoints

Goes with: Sammy Meets Amy the Amygdala, Allah Never Sleeps, So I Can, The Trap Detectives

For families of teenagers

Words That Build Bridges

Who it's for
Parents and their teens, or parents on their own
Length
60 to 90 minutes with Q&A
Format
In person or online

The teen years can make conversations feel harder, just when they matter most. Rooted in the gentle, honest way the Prophet ﷺ spoke with young people, this workshop focuses on the words, tone and timing that keep families talking.

What we'll explore

  • Listening first, and why tone matters as much as words
  • Talking about growing up, haya and the body as an amanah
  • Disagreeing without breaking the connection
  • Making room for your teen's questions about faith
  • Small daily habits that keep the door open

Goes with: What Nobody Told Me About Growing Up, You're All Grown Up. Now What?
